18 U.S.C. § 2257 RECORD-KEEPING COMPLIANCE STATEMENT

Statutory Notice to visitors, creators, and third parties

1. Studio Exemption & Primary Producer Status

Cre8tor House Studios is a luxury physical production facility, workspace, and community incubator. Cre8tor House Studios is not a primary producer, publisher, or distributor of sexually explicit visual depictions as defined under 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. Part 75.

All content produced by independent creators within Cre8tor House Studios facilities is created, owned, uploaded, and distributed solely by the respective independent creator on their chosen third-party platforms (e.g. OnlyFans, Fansly). Each creator acts as their own primary producer responsible for maintaining all individual 2257 record-keeping files.

2. Age Verification Mandate

As a matter of strict company policy, all models and creators entering Cre8tor House Studios must be at least 21 years of age and must present valid, unexpired government-issued photo identification prior to accessing studio sets.
