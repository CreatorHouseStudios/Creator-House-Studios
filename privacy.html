PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Commitment to Creator Privacy

Cre8tor House Studios ("we," "our," or "us") understands the paramount importance of privacy and personal safety for creators in the adult digital economy. We are strictly committed to safeguarding your legal identity, contact numbers, and confidential submission records.

2. Information We Collect

  • Application Data: Legal full name, stage name, email address, telephone number, date of birth, location, social/content platforms, earnings brackets, and personal creator goals.
  • Identity Verification: For applicants progressing to residency interviews, government-issued photo ID (driver's license or passport) to verify that all residents are 21 years of age or older in compliance with federal statutes.
  • Technical Data: Standard access logs (browser type, IP address, timestamps).

3. How Your Information is Protected

We will never sell, rent, or publicly disclose your real legal identity, personal contact details, or location to marketing brokers, advertisers, or third parties. All application submissions are stored in encrypted environments accessible solely by authorized management.

4. Retention & Purging

Unaccepted applicant submissions are purged from active evaluation records within 90 days. Records for confirmed residents are maintained in strict confidentiality under applicable compliance guidelines.
